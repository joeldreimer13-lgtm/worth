About these spending numbers

Net posted spending from your connected accounts. Account transfers and card payments are excluded; refunds reduce spending on their posted date. Pending transactions are excluded. Categories follow your financial feed.

Confirmed cash expenses can be recorded as separate adjustments. The app adds each adjustment once, using its unique ID, without changing the original bank-feed totals.

New data appears after you import records. This app does not connect to banks or upload records. It runs entirely in your browser, served by GitHub Pages.

What’s included ↗

Cash and savings, minus credit cards. Retirement, vehicles, physical cash, and unconnected accounts are outside the net-worth chart. Keep the same scope for every imported snapshot.

This is the recorded balance history. A transfer may reach one account before leaving another, so short-lived spikes can reflect reporting timing. There is no reliable intraday history: the cursor snaps to a recorded date instead of estimating values.
